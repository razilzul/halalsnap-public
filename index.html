FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is HalalSnap free?

Yes. Every member gets free scan credits each week, and you can earn more by inviting friends or leaving a review. An optional premium plan helps fund free access for the community.

How does HalalSnap work?

Point your camera at a product's ingredient list (or pick a photo). In seconds, our AI reads every ingredient, weighs it against Islamic jurisprudence, and returns a clear verdict with plain-language reasoning.

What can I scan with HalalSnap?

Food and beverages, cosmetics and beauty, dental and oral care, supplements and vitamins, personal care, and more — each analysed with considerations tuned to its category.

Does HalalSnap support different madhhabs?

Yes. Choose Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali, or General, and the guidance adapts to that school's principles while noting where scholars differ.

Is HalalSnap available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. HalalSnap is free to download on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Does HalalSnap replace official halal certification?

No. HalalSnap offers AI-assisted guidance, not a religious ruling. For important or doubtful cases, always verify with a qualified scholar or official halal certification.
