Why it depends

HalalSnap has no specific rule for E160e, so a scan does not single it out. Singapore's MUIS flags it in its 2016 certifier list: certifiers ask the maker to document its source or process. That is a check for certifiers, not a ruling on every product that contains it.

How to check the pack

  • Look for a recognised halal mark (JAKIM, MUI/BPJPH, MUIS or another reputable certifier) on the pack in your hand. A mark you can see settles it for the whole product.R-CERT-01
  • A vegan or vegetarian mark settles animal-source questions.R-CERT-05
  • A kosher mark rules out pork only. It does not settle the halal question on its own.R-CERT-04
  • Still unsure? Ask the maker which source they use, or choose a certified alternative.
