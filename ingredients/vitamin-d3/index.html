What vitamin D3 is

Vitamin D3 is added to milk, cereals and supplements. Food-grade D3 is usually made from lanolin, the grease in sheep's wool, or synthetically.

Why it depends

Wool from a living animal is pure in the majority position, and halal-certified lanolin D3 is widely available. A minority of scholars still treat it as doubtful, and only a certifier can confirm one maker's exact supplier, which is why the answer is "likely" rather than certified. An otherwise plain product is never made doubtful over added vitamins alone.

How to check the pack

  • A halal mark settles it. "Vegan D3" from lichen avoids the question.
  • Look for a recognised halal mark (JAKIM, MUI/BPJPH, MUIS or another reputable certifier) on the pack in your hand. A mark you can see settles it for the whole product.R-CERT-01
  • Still unsure? Ask the maker which source they use, or choose a certified alternative.
