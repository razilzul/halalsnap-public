Is gelatin (E441) halal?

Gelatin · Gelatine

The short answer
Doubtful until the source is shown

Doubtful when the label does not say where the gelatin came from. Pork gelatin is haram, fish and plant alternatives are halal, and beef gelatin needs halal slaughter.

What gelatin (E441) is

Gelatin is a gelling agent made from animal collagen, from pig or cattle skin and bones, or from fish. It sets gummies, marshmallows, jelly and some yoghurts, and coats capsules.

By mazhab

Why it depends

Gelatine is made from animal collagen. Pork gelatine is haram in every school. Fish gelatine is halal. Beef or chicken gelatine needs the same halal-slaughter support as the meat. Gelatine with no source given, in something you eat, is doubtful. Kosher gelatine is not automatically halal.

How to check the pack

  • A halal mark printed on the pack, "fish gelatine", or a plant gelling agent such as pectin, agar or carrageenan instead. "Beef gelatine" still needs halal slaughter.
  • Look for a recognised halal mark (JAKIM, MUI/BPJPH, MUIS or another reputable certifier) on the pack in your hand. A mark you can see settles it for the whole product.R-CERT-01
  • A vegan or vegetarian mark settles animal-source questions.R-CERT-05
  • A kosher mark rules out pork only. It does not settle the halal question on its own.R-CERT-04
  • Still unsure? Ask the maker which source they use, or choose a certified alternative.
