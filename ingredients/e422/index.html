Is E422 halal?

Glycerin · Glycerol

The short answer
Doubtful until the source is shown

A bare "E422" on a label is doubtful until the pack shows where it came from. What settles it: a halal mark printed on the pack, a vegan or vegetarian mark, or a plant source written next to it (for example "vegetable" or "soy").

Why it depends

Glycerin is made from vegetable oils, animal fats or synthetically, and the finished glycerin looks the same. "Vegetable glycerin" settles it. A bare "glycerin" or "E422" does not.

How to check the pack

  • A halal mark printed on the pack, a vegan or vegetarian mark, or a plant source written next to it (for example "vegetable" or "soy").
  • Look for a recognised halal mark (JAKIM, MUI/BPJPH, MUIS or another reputable certifier) on the pack in your hand. A mark you can see settles it for the whole product.R-CERT-01
  • A vegan or vegetarian mark settles animal-source questions.R-CERT-05
  • A kosher mark rules out pork only. It does not settle the halal question on its own.R-CERT-04
  • The source has to sit right next to the additive. "Palm oil" earlier in the list does not tell you where a later bare emulsifier came from.R-GUARD-01
  • Still unsure? Ask the maker which source they use, or choose a certified alternative.
