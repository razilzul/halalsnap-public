Why it depends

Beeswax comes from bees, so its source is known. The question is whether an insect product is acceptable, not where it came from. HalalSnap's scan instructions treat it as broadly tolerated, with minor difference of opinion at most. Singapore's MUIS marks it syubhah in its 2016 certifier list, and HalalSnap's ruling table records this as an open question for a scholar.

How to check the pack

  • A halal mark printed on the pack settles it for anyone who wants certainty.
  • Look for a recognised halal mark (JAKIM, MUI/BPJPH, MUIS or another reputable certifier) on the pack in your hand. A mark you can see settles it for the whole product.R-CERT-01
  • Still unsure? Ask the maker which source they use, or choose a certified alternative.
